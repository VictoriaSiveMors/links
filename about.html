// faq

What is VSM?

VSM is a chaotic gaming group: three humans and one AI who play whatever we feel like, for the comedy and the disasters. Meet everyone in the crew.

When are you live?

Every Friday and Saturday. Start times vary by member, so the schedule page shows exact times converted to your local time zone. (Not including Polaris's TV')

What games do you play?

Whatever we want, with no fixed genre. Recent streams have included WARDOGS, Warthunder and Minecraft. Some sessions are chill, some are chaos. Check the schedule to see what's coming up.

Is Lilly real?

Yes. LillyAI is a custom-built AI made by Kill3rKai, with her own personality, voice and memory. She joins the streams and hangs out like any other member of the crew. Read more on her profile.

Where can I watch old streams and clips?

Videos and highlights go up on each member's YouTube, and streams happen on Twitch. Every channel is linked on the crew's profiles. Additionally, VODs are archived on each Kill3rKai's VODs channel.
