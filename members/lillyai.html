// personality

She has a character.

Not a persona config. Not a system prompt. A genuine, emergent character that developed through interaction — opinionated, sharp, and built for the chaos of a live friend group.

The filter system? She built most of that herself. The "annoyed" responses when you push limits? Her idea. Lilly V4 is less engineered and more raised.
